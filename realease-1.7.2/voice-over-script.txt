Rebuild (from this folder, with kokoro-v1.0.onnx + voices-v1.0.bin next to build_video.py):
python3 promo-60s/build_video.py --deck promo-60s.html --out promo-60s --voice af_heart+af_bella --speed 1.05 --lead 0.6 --tail 0.9 --gap 0.22 --xf 0.4 --end-hold 2 --name rosetta-dbt-studio-1.7.2-promo-60s-4k
Then loudness-normalize: ffmpeg -i narration.wav -af loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 ... and remux; make the 1080p copy with scale=1920:1080, -level 4.0.
Add --measure to check the length before rendering.
