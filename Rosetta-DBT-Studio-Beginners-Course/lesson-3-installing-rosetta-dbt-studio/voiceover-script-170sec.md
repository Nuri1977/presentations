# Installing Rosetta DBT Studio - Voiceover

Slide 1 - Intro

Let's get Rosetta DBT Studio installed. Windows first, then Mac, so skip ahead to whichever one you are on.

There is nothing to set up beforehand, and no terminal to open.

Slide 2 - Windows, download

Head to rosettadb.io, and click Download Now. Then Choose your build.

Click Download for Windows. That is the only file you need.

Slide 3 - Windows, install

Open the installer and let it run.

Nothing to choose, no options to set. It brings its own Python, so nothing else to install.

Slide 4 - Windows, first launch

Leave every adapter ticked, and click Install.

It installs dbt and every adapter. This takes a couple of minutes.

Slide 5 - Windows, setup complete

Then two confirmations. Python installed, and dbt installed. Click Finish.

Slide 6 - Windows, quick tour

Give it a moment, and Rosetta DBT Studio opens for the first time.

Last thing. It offers you a quick tour of this screen, showing where your projects live, how to start one, and where your database connections are kept.

Slide 7 - Mac divider

That is Windows done. Now let's install it on a Mac.

Slide 8 - Mac, download

Head to rosettadb.io, and click Download Now. Then Choose your build.

Under macOS there are two builds. One for Apple Silicon, and one for Intel. Pick the one that matches your Mac, and it downloads through your browser like any other file.

Slide 9 - Mac, install

Open it when the download finishes, and the installer window appears.

Drag Rosetta DBT Studio into your Applications folder. That is the entire install. Nothing to click through, nothing to choose.

Slide 10 - Mac, first open

Now open your Applications folder, and start Rosetta DBT Studio from there.

First launch takes a moment, because it is fetching Rosetta and unpacking Python before it opens.

Slide 11 - Mac, the setup wizard

Now the setup. Python is already there, so it only needs dbt and the adapters.

Leave them all ticked, and click Install. This takes a couple of minutes.

When it finishes, click Next.

Slide 12 - Mac, setup complete

Two confirmations. Python installed, and dbt installed. Now click Finish, and it restarts itself, which is meant to happen.

Slide 13 - Mac, quick tour

Give it a moment, and Rosetta DBT Studio opens for the first time.

Last thing. It offers you a quick tour of this screen, showing where your projects live and how to start one.

Slide 14 - Closing

That is Rosetta DBT Studio installed and set up. Next up, a proper look at the interface, and what everything on screen does.
