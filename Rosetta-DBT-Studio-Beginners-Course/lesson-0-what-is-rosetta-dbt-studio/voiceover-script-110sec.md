# What is Rosetta DBT Studio? - Voiceover

Slide 1 - Hook

If you work in data, your tools are probably scattered. One thing to move data. Another to transform it. Something else to look at what you've actually got. And a separate place to run it all on a schedule.

Rosetta DBT Studio is all of that, in one place.

Slide 2 - One workspace

It's a desktop app that brings a data engineer's tools into a single, cohesive workspace.

Your connections, your data, your models, your pipelines all in one window. And it's AI-first throughout.

Slide 3 - What's inside

Inside, it bundles the pieces you would otherwise install and wire together yourself.

RosettaDB, for extracting, migrating and testing data. dbt Core, for transformation. DuckDB, a modern database for local and cloud analytics. And DuckLake, to build your own data lake on the storage you already use.

On top of those: Cloud Explorer, a SQL editor with notebooks, Flowfile for visual pipelines, and Rosetta Cloud.

Slide 4 - Discover

So you can see your data wherever it lives. Sitting in cloud storage, or in a database.

Browse it, and preview it - without loading it somewhere else first.

Slide 5 - Analyze

When you want to dig in, there's a full SQL editor and notebooks.

You can query and analyze any of it, no matter where it's stored.

Slide 6 - Move and orchestrate

It also handles getting your data in, and keeping it moving. You can build a pipeline visually, as a graph of steps, or write it out as code, whichever you prefer.

Slide 7 - Scale

And when your data grows too big for your laptop, the same project runs in the cloud instead, on far more powerful hardware. You don't have to change a thing.

Slide 8 - Closing

That's Rosetta DBT Studio.

At the centre of it is dbt, and in the next video, we'll look at what dbt actually is.
