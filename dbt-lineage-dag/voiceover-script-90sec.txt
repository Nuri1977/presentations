This is the Lineage view.

Open a model file, click the Lineage tab at the bottom,
and Rosetta renders the graph instantly.

Raw sources on the left. Your model in the center.
Downstream consumers and tests on the right.

Use the Depth selector to control how many hops to show —
1, 2, 3, or 4 levels deep.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


Click any node to inspect it.

The details panel shows you the model description,
what it depends on, and its columns.

You can see exactly where the data comes from
and what depends on this model downstream.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


Click Load Lineage to go even deeper.

Rosetta maps column-level lineage —
showing exactly which upstream columns feed into each output column.

Trace your data from raw source to final mart,
column by column.
